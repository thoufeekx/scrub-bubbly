Support

Need a hand? Here are quick answers to the most common questions. You can also email us any time.

How do I play?

Drag your finger to scrub the mold away and clean the whole surface to finish the level. Watch out for the surprises: mold that grows back, speed rushes, root spots you have to hold, grime leaks and spore puffs.

I can't feel the vibration

Check that System Haptics is on in Settings > Sounds & Haptics, and that haptics are switched on in the game's settings (the gear button).

There's no sound

Turn off the silent switch or Silent Mode, raise the volume, and check that sound is on in the game's settings.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
