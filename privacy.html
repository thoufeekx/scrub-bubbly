Scrub Bubbly ASMR

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Scrub Bubbly is a cleaning game you play offline. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data, and the game has no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't send your personal information, usage data, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or analytics to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars and settings (sound, haptics, tool and colour choices) are saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app can post your total stars to a leaderboard. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple's own privacy policy covers it. We never see your Apple ID, email or contacts. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device's Settings.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases today. If we add any, Apple processes the payment and we never receive your card or payment details. We'll update this policy before that happens.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

Tracking

We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask permission for.

Security

The game runs entirely on your device. It has no servers holding your data and no user accounts that could be breached. The app uses only Apple's standard system frameworks.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
